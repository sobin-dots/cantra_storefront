## Brand Guidelines

### Color Scheme

**Primary Colors:**

- Primary Dark: `#343F52` – Primary headings, strong emphasis, dark sections
- Accent Green: `#00A579` – Primary CTA buttons, links, highlights
- Background Light: `#FEFEFE` – Main page background
- Text Primary: `#00A579` – Key highlighted text
- Link Color: `#00A579` – Hyperlinks and interactive elements

**Usage Rules:**

- Use `#00A579` for primary CTAs and actionable elements.
- Use `#343F52` for headings and structured content.
- Maintain clean white (`#FEFEFE`) backgrounds for clarity and professionalism.
- Avoid introducing additional accent colors outside the system.

---

### Typography

**Primary Font Family:** Space Grotesk  
**Fallback:** sans-serif

**Font Usage:**

- **Headings (H1 – 44px):** Space Grotesk, bold
- **Subheadings (H2 – 26px):** Space Grotesk, semi-bold
- **Body Text (19px):** Space Grotesk, regular
- All typography should maintain modern spacing and strong readability.

**Font Stacks:**

- Headings: `"Space Grotesk", sans-serif`
- Body: `"Space Grotesk", sans-serif`
- Paragraphs: `"Space Grotesk", sans-serif`

- Text Color: `#00A579`
